1B particle step: time by timestep bin

Where the wall time of one maximum timestep goes, by timestep bin, from the leapfrog Times column of a 128-rank 1B run.

Times: each substep's wall time is added to the coarsest bin that is synchronised at its end, so bin k (k ≥ 1) collects 2k−1 substeps and ms per substep is minutes / substeps. Particle counts are the distribution at the end of the step; bins that were occupied only part of the step show time with no particles, and no substep count. Gas and non-gas columns are local to rank 0. The first time around (all particles) is not in any bin.
