Phantom on GPU

Phantom’s density and force calculations running on a single GPU through cosmoSPHere, with periodic boundaries, cooling, disc viscosity and a choice of cubic or quintic kernel. Three pages cover how fast it is, where the time goes, and how closely it agrees with Phantom on CPU.

The two benchmarks

  • Shamrock paper (David-Cléris, Laibe & Lapeyre 2025, MNRAS 539, 1; arXiv:2503.09713). The Sedov time-stepping benchmark is its §5.2.3, and the 1.37M-particle point is the third point of its Fig. 17.
  • Shamrock homogeneous SPH benchmark script, the 2²⁵-particle benchmark, in the Shamrock repository.
  • Shamrock’s published results for that benchmark (Shamrock documentation), with the raw results file giving particle counts, compilers and dates.
